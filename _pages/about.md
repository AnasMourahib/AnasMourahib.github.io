I am a postdoctoral researcher and lecturer in statistics at [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://www.tue.nl/en/). My research focuses on causal inference, graphical models, spatial and spatio-temporal statistics, and multivariate extremes. It combines structural causal models and the potential-outcomes framework with statistical learning methods for complex real-world data.

I currently work on root-cause analysis in collaboration with ASML, a world-leading semiconductor manufacturer, and on the causal effect of protected areas on conflict in Madagascar. I am also a lecturer for the course *Dependence Modeling: Graphical Models and Causality*. My broader interests include scalable methods for assessing interventions with observational spatial and spatio-temporal data.

I obtained my PhD in Statistics at [UCLouvain](https://uclouvain.be/en/) in 2025, under the supervision of Anna Kiriliouk and Johan Segers. My thesis, *Multivariate threshold exceedance approaches in the presence of multiple extreme directions and applications*, is available [here](https://research.dial.uclouvain.be/entities/publication/d00183ae-949a-4640-896e-b98e22286332).

Research interests
======
- Causal inference and root-cause analysis
- Causal inference for spatial conflict data
- Treatment-effect estimation and intervention assessment
- Extreme-value theory and risk modeling
- Probabilistic graphical models
