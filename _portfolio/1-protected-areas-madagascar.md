Collaboration with Faniry Razakason and Julia Fischer, started at the StatML–Bocconi Summer School on causality. Madagascar is well known for its biodiversity, and many initiatives protect areas of high biodiversity, for example by creating national parks. However, many local inhabitants depend on these areas for their livelihood, for instance through agriculture or hunting, which can create conflict. The aim of the project is to quantify the causal effect of protected areas on conflict.
