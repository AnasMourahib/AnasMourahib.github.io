Joint work with Alexander Mey, Ignacio Echave-Sustaeta and Rui Pires da Silva Castro, in collaboration with ASML. The project develops data-driven methods to identify and diagnose root causes in complex industrial systems, using statistical learning, causal inference and dependence modeling.
